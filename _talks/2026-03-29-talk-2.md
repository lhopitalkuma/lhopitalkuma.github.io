After reading the book "Tatami Galaxy", I felt so touched. This book is a good mirror of campus life -- you seek for colorful daily, various activities, success in acadamic or romantic. However, you will definitely meet failure and feel a waste of life. You may regret, you may wanna have another try. Finally you know rosy-life is just fantasy, there is not rosy-life just because the world is not rosy. You are yourself, your life is yours, not others.
